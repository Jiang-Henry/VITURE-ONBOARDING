Welcome Aboard, Viturist,

Meet your team

VITURE is a global pioneer and leader in the Extended Reality (XR) glasses industry, revolutionizing immersive experiences and shaping the next-generation gateway to digital content through cutting-edge technology, sleek design, and user-centric innovation.

With a commitment to excellence, VITURE has introduced a range of groundbreaking products, including XR glasses, neckband computers, and related accessories, alongside software solutions such as SpaceWalker and Immersive 3D. These innovations are designed to deliver an unparalleled product experience for consumers worldwide.

3. 价值观

客观、乐观

  • 第一性原理：努力掌握更接近真实的信息，并进行无损传递，不人云亦云
  • 小ego：乐于接受批评和建议，对不同观点保持开放和好奇，愿意在事实面前随时改变自己的观点
  • 乐观：保持积极的心态，遇到困难和冲突时能够控制情绪、积极的面对

创业精神

  • 光速：以理论的最快速度行动，科技行业的速度是赢得竞争的核心因素，慢鱼会被快鱼吃掉
  • 知行合一：没有人是100%的管理者，每个人都需要深入一线的工作，包括CEO
  • “Find a way”：利用现有的资源尽最大可能解决问题，而不是直接"Say no"或等待“完美条件”

追求极致

  • 用户至上：一切围绕用户体验，不画蛇添足
  • Medicore is not an option：只有最好的才是有价值的，成为第二好不可接受

Getting started

Day 1

  • Join Wi‑Fi / VPN and confirm access works.
  • Set up your company email + calendar.
  • Install required tools (Slack / Notion / Google Drive, etc.).
  • Meet your buddy + manager for first goals.

Week 1

  • Get added to key channels and mailing lists.
  • Read product overview and brand basics.
  • Run through security + device setup.
  • Do your first small deliverable.

Month 1

  • Own a project area and share weekly updates.
  • Meet stakeholders across functions.
  • Align on expectations & growth plan.
  • Ship something meaningful.
